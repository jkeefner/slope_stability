🪨 Material Properties
φ(σn) = φ₀ − Δφ · log₁₀(σn / pₐ)
Leps (1970) nonlinear envelope for rockfill and waste rock. Friction angle decreases with increasing normal stress. pₐ = atmospheric pressure (2,116 psf). In multi-bench mode, each segment's φ' serves as φ₀ for that segment.
💧 Hydrogeology
Straight phreatic surface, depth below ground at the toe and at the crest (linearly interpolated, clamped to the ground surface). Equal depths ≡ a horizontal water table.
0.0-0.1: Well-drained/Depressurized
0.1-0.2: Partial drainage
0.2-0.3: Moderate pore pressure
0.3-0.5: High pore pressure
Combined mode: Water table defines phreatic surface; Ru applies additional pore pressure below it
🌐 Seismic (Pseudo-static)
Pseudo-static horizontal force kh·W toward the slope face. 0 = static. A common screening value is kh ≈ ½·PGA(g).
🪨 Tension Crack
Auto-placed on the crest side at the most critical depth; water fill adds hydrostatic thrust on the crack face (0 = dry, 100% = full). Steep surfaces crack automatically at the active-wedge angle even when this is off.
⚙️ Search Constraints
Non-circular runs the circular search first, then a surface-altering optimization that can follow weak seams and form a tension crack (reported via Janbu, robust).

Search Constraint Geometry
Max Depth: Maximum vertical distance from slope surface to failure arc. Shallower depths find bench-scale or surficial failures.
Min/Max Radius: Circle radius bounds. Smaller radii = tighter, shallower arcs. Larger radii = deeper, more sweeping failures.
Entry Zone: Where the failure surface enters the slope (toe area). Positive values move upslope from toe.
Exit Zone: Where the failure surface exits (crest area). Negative values move downslope from crest.
Bench: Shallow, single-bench failures. Max depth ~25% H, small radius range.
Inter-Ramp: Mid-depth failures spanning multiple benches. Max depth ~50% H.
Overall: Deep-seated failures through entire slope. Max depth ~100% H or more.
